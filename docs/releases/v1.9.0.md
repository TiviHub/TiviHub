## TiviHub v1.9.0

This release gives TiviHub a new look.

### Highlights

- A new terminal-style theme: black, grey and purple, monospace type, square corners and flat colours, with no gradients or glows.
- A new flat purple app icon for the taskbar, installer and website.
- Lists, panels and selected items share one style: grey rows with a purple bar for the selected item.
- Tooltips now appear only on buttons that have an icon and no text.
- The page labels above headings are gone.
- The website was redesigned, and its app previews now match the app.

### Platform and privacy

- Windows 10/11 (x64) desktop installer using WebView2.
- Linux x64 AppImage and `.deb`, plus macOS arm64 and x64 `.dmg` packages.
- Playlists, credentials, catalogs, favorites, playback progress, settings, and diagnostic history stay on the device. TiviHub has no application server or account system.
- Playback, catalog, guide, and subtitle requests go directly from the app to the configured provider or service.

Use only sources and content you are authorized to access and follow applicable law and provider terms.
