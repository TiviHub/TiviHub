## TiviHub v1.4.0

This release adds a full Live TV guide and refines playback, casting, and catalog navigation.

### Highlights

- Browse all channels on a day-based EPG timeline with a current-time marker, channel groups and favorites, channel and programme search, zoom, and compact rows.
- Keep the selected-channel guide available alongside the player, with the full timeline available on demand.
- Load Xtream short-guide data in bounded batches for larger channel lists. Guide availability and coverage still depend on the provider's EPG data.
- Improve casting status and stop-casting controls. Keep playback in an in-app mini-player when navigating away from Live TV.
- Try native HLS playback for compatible, non-DRM streams when Shaka reports the supported fallback errors, including HTTP 509.
- Improve sanitized playback diagnostics and clarify temporary stream-server errors.
- Refine desktop Live TV sizing, responsive guide controls, and catalog layouts. Pin a shorter top navigation over scrolling content and keep the gradient in the content area. Make the recently added Movies and Series shelf follow active filters and sorting.
- Remove the unfinished MPV preview path and native browser Picture-in-Picture in favor of the in-app player controls.

### Known limitations

- EPG listings, including future-day coverage, depend on what the configured provider supplies; empty rows do not necessarily indicate a player problem.
- Playback compatibility depends on the provider, stream format, DRM, and WebView codecs. The native HLS fallback is limited to supported non-DRM streams.

### Platform and privacy

- Windows 10/11 (x64) desktop installer using WebView2. Windows is the only released platform; Linux and macOS are not supported.
- Playlists, credentials, catalogs, favorites, playback progress, settings, and diagnostic history stay on the device. TiviHub has no application server or account system.
- Playback, catalog, guide, and subtitle requests go directly from the app to the configured provider or service.

Use only sources and content you are authorized to access and follow applicable law and provider terms.
