## TiviHub v1.10.0

This release makes the app look and behave the same everywhere.

### Highlights

- Loading states now use skeleton placeholders instead of spinners: catalog grids, episodes, downloads, ratings, the TV Guide, subtitle and cast searches, and the player start.
- Movie and series pages have a full-width poster header with one play button and round icon actions.
- Library, favorites, episodes, downloads and Live TV channels share one card style, with the same hover and selected states.
- Icons, buttons, dropdowns, search fields, page margins and hover speed use one size each throughout the app, including Settings and the TV Guide.
- Toasts and the "Playlist expired" notice share one shape and sit in the screen corners.
- The global search dialog and topbar search match the other search fields.

### Platform and privacy

- Windows 10/11 (x64) desktop installer using WebView2.
- Linux x64 AppImage and `.deb`, plus macOS arm64 and x64 `.dmg` packages.
- Playlists, credentials, catalogs, favorites, playback progress, settings, and diagnostic history stay on the device. TiviHub has no application server or account system.
- Playback, catalog, guide, and subtitle requests go directly from the app to the configured provider or service.

Use only sources and content you are authorized to access and follow applicable law and provider terms.
