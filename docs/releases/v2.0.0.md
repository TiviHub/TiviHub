## TiviHub v2.0.0

This release rebuilds how you browse (by category, a batch at a time), adds more ways to watch and connect, protects your saved logins, and lets you download much larger videos.

### Highlights

- Browse by category: Live TV, Movies and Series open on a list of categories, and a category's titles load a batch at a time as you scroll, so a provider with hundreds of thousands of titles opens at once instead of after a long import. Adding or refreshing an Xtream or Stalker source now imports only the live channels; movies and series are read when you open them. Search, A–Z sorting and category counts work in every category, and counts appear only when they are known. Titles you have seen stay on your device, so favorites, library, Home and search find them again.
- Live TV is one screen: categories, channels and the player side by side, with a TV guide for the playing channel under the picture. Library, TV Guide, Downloads and Settings use the same two-panel layout.
- Every search box has an X that empties it.
- Loading placeholders now sit exactly where the content will appear, so nothing jumps when it arrives.
- Open in VLC or mpv (desktop app): a button in the player hands the stream to VLC or mpv when the built-in player cannot play it. When a file shows a picture but its sound cannot be decoded (AC-3 or E-AC-3 audio), TiviHub now says so instead of leaving you with silence.
- Stalker (Ministra) portals (desktop app): add a portal with its address and the MAC address your provider gave you to get live channels, movies, series and a short TV guide. Stream addresses are requested when you press play, because portals only keep them for a short time. Series are included, whether the portal keeps them in its video library or in a series library of its own. A portal gives out 14 titles per request, so adding one imports only the live channels, and movies and series are read a category at a time (four requests at a time, so the portal does not throttle you). On a large portal the movie and series category counts fill in over a minute or two after you open the section; until then a category shows no number. Search and A–Z sorting are done by the portal, so Z–A is not offered for portal movies and series. Ratings work for portal movies when the portal gives a year (or the name has one).
- Custom User-Agent per playlist (desktop app): some providers only accept certain apps. Set one in the playlist's optional settings and TiviHub uses it for the playlist, the guide, downloads and playback. Casting to a Chromecast still uses the stream address directly.
- Radio mode: stations marked `radio="true"` in the playlist (or in a group called Radio) get a Radio filter in Live TV and play as audio, with the station logo in place of the picture.
- Catch-up TV: on channels whose provider keeps recordings (Xtream archive, or M3U `catchup` playlists), finished programmes in the TV guide are marked with a bar and open the recording. The programme on air has a Start over button.
- Playlists with `rtmp://`, `rtsp://` or `udp://` entries now import. Those entries cannot play in TiviHub, so they are left out, and the message after importing tells you how many were skipped.
- Favorites and resume points on M3U playlists stay on the right title when the provider adds or removes entries. Refreshing a playlist now keeps the existing entries' ids instead of renumbering them.
- Offline downloads are saved in small pieces instead of one block in memory, so large films no longer crash the window. The limit is now 8 GB per video (it was 2 GB). Downloads from earlier versions still play.
- The add-source form warns when a playlist address starts with `http://`, because the login is then readable by anyone on your network.
- Refreshing a playlist with many entries that share one stream address is much faster.

### Removed

- The popular-catalog (Top 5) rows on Movies and Series are gone. Ratings, summaries and the other MDBList features stay.
- "Check all channels in a group" in Live TV is gone.

### Security

- A temporary problem with the system keychain (locked, busy) can no longer cause TiviHub to create a new encryption key and lock you out of your saved logins. TiviHub now shows an error and asks you to restart instead.
- The desktop app now restricts which scripts and connections its window may use (Content Security Policy). Playback, images and Chromecast still reach your provider and devices.

### Under the hood

- The app's main screen code is split into smaller parts (notifications, updates, downloads, library, local data, playback, sources and search), so future changes are safer.
- Lint rules for unused code and error causes are now enforced in CI.

### Platform and privacy

- Upgrading adds one small cache table to the local database (the last answer per category). It is created automatically, nothing existing is changed, and the cache can be deleted at any time.

- Windows 10/11 (x64) desktop installer using WebView2.
- Linux x64 AppImage and `.deb`, plus macOS arm64 and x64 `.dmg` packages.
- Playlists, credentials, catalogs, favorites, playback progress, settings, and diagnostic history stay on the device. TiviHub has no application server or account system.
- Playback, catalog, guide, and subtitle requests go directly from the app to the configured provider or service.

Use only sources and content you are authorized to access and follow applicable law and provider terms.
