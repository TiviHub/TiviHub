## TiviHub v2.1.0

This release brings TiviHub in seven languages and adds a PIN for adult content, TV guide reminders and a multiview for live channels.

### Highlights

- Seven languages: English, Dutch, Spanish, French, German, Portuguese and Turkish. TiviHub starts in your system language when it is one of these, and you can change it under Settings > Appearance. Dates, numbers and "1 video / 2 videos" follow the language too. The legal notice and the diagnostic report you can share with support stay in English, and so do error messages that come from deep inside the desktop app.
- PIN for adult content: under Settings > Adult content you can set a PIN of 4 to 8 digits. Adult content then stays hidden until you enter it, and it locks again every time TiviHub starts. After five wrong tries you wait 30 seconds. This is a child lock, not strong security: someone who can read the files on your computer can remove it. The PIN is not part of a backup, and restoring a backup cannot turn adult content on while a PIN is set.
- TV guide reminders: a bell on programmes that have not started sets a reminder. One minute before the start TiviHub shows a message, and a Windows notification when the window is not in front (the desktop app asks for permission the first time you set a reminder). TiviHub has to be open to remind you. A reminder that was due while TiviHub was closed is dropped, and you are told how many you missed. Clicking the notification does not open the channel yet.
- Multiview (desktop and browser): the new button in the Live TV channel list opens up to four live channels side by side. One has sound; click the speaker on another tile to switch. You pick channels from the list Live TV was showing. Opening multiview stops the player you were watching. Channels with DRM protection and channels from a Stalker portal cannot be shown in multiview. Many providers allow only one or two connections per account, so extra tiles may fail to load.

### Under the hood

- Reminders add one small table to the local database (schema version 4; the browser copy moves to version 9). It is created automatically and nothing existing is changed. Reminders are not part of backups, and restoring a backup removes them.

### Platform and privacy

- Reminders add the system notification permission to the desktop app. Nothing is sent anywhere: notifications are shown by your computer.
- The language and the PIN are saved on this device only.
