## TiviHub v2.1.1

This release adds a reminders bell to the top bar, so you can see and manage your TV guide reminders.

### Highlights

- Reminders bell: a bell in the top bar lists your upcoming reminders and the ones that already went off, including those you missed while TiviHub was closed. A dot shows what you have not looked at yet. You can remove a single reminder or clear the list. The list keeps the 20 most recent reminders that went off.

### Under the hood

- Reminders that went off are now kept in the local database instead of being deleted, so the bell can list them. No schema change; reminders are still not part of backups.
