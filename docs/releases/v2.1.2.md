## TiviHub v2.1.2

This release keeps your place when you move around the app, and makes buttons, fields and text look the same everywhere.

### Highlights

- Keep your place: Live TV, Movies and Series remember the category you chose, your search, the sort order, how far you scrolled in both lists and, in Live TV, the selected channel. Open a movie or series and go back, or visit another page and return, and you are where you were. Switching to another source starts fresh.
- Back to top: long lists (categories, channels, movies and series) show a button once you have scrolled far down that takes you back to the top.
- Saved series open: a series in your Library no longer sits greyed out; it opens its page like a favorite does. A saved movie or episode that has no stored stream address is still greyed out.
- One look for fields: every search box and input has the same single purple frame when you click in it, instead of two frames at different heights.
- The back button on movie and series pages now matches the other action buttons there.
- Light theme: progress bars, loading placeholders and form fields are lighter and match the rest.

### Under the hood

- Buttons and fields share fixed heights, and small text uses four sizes. The smallest labels (badges and counts) are a little bigger than before.
