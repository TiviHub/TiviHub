## TiviHub v1.11.0

This release gives the app a softer, more modern look and improves the player.

### Highlights

- A new look: Roboto type, small 3px corners everywhere, and a flat background instead of the dot grid. The website follows the same look.
- The player has a cleaner layout: the video runs edge to edge, live channels carry a "Live" chip, and the tools sit in one row below it.
- Subtitle search in the player uses the same search field and list rows as the rest of the app. Downloaded subtitles are saved per title and kept on the device until you clear them, so a subtitle you downloaded once never costs another request.
- Home shows a Resume card for the title you were last watching.
- Library tabs have icons, and each row shows its type and how much you have watched.
- Downloads show how much space your saved videos use and what is still in progress.
- The catalog shows how many titles match your search and category, with a Clear filters link.
- TV Guide: the empty message is visible, the controls wrap on tablets, and the date shows "Today".
- Live TV on phones no longer overlaps the Channels and Favorites lists.

### Platform and privacy

- Windows 10/11 (x64) desktop installer using WebView2.
- Linux x64 AppImage and `.deb`, plus macOS arm64 and x64 `.dmg` packages.
- Playlists, credentials, catalogs, favorites, playback progress, settings, and diagnostic history stay on the device. TiviHub has no application server or account system.
- Playback, catalog, guide, and subtitle requests go directly from the app to the configured provider or service.

Use only sources and content you are authorized to access and follow applicable law and provider terms.
